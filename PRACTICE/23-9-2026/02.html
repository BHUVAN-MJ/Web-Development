How to make a tea

Tea is a drink.That most of the us will drink atleast once a day

  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Tea leaf/Tea powder
  1. Take a pot and put the milk into the pot and start to boil the milk
  2. Take the Sugar and put it in the boiling milk.Put the sugar as much you want but don't put too much.
  3. Last but not least add the Tea powder/leaf. i recoment to put some
  4. Wait for some time.There you have it your Tea.
